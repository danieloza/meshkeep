# Postmortem: payment confirmations delayed

Date: 14 March 2026 (staging and the pilot shop)
Duration: 47 minutes, from 09:12 to 09:59
Severity: high

## Impact

63 orders stayed in "awaiting payment" although the customers had paid. No money was lost. Eleven customers contacted support.

## Root cause

The TLS certificate on the webhook endpoint expired at 09:12. The payment operator refused to deliver confirmations to an endpoint with an invalid certificate.

## Detection

A customer report at 09:31. No alert fired, because nothing watched the certificate.

## Fix

Tomek renewed the certificate at 09:55. The operator redelivered the queued confirmations within four minutes.
